# Profiles

## Tour

Use for a complete workflow. Target 45–90 seconds and usually 6–10 scenes. Preserve explanation and reading time rather than compressing every feature.

## Launch

Use for a short product reveal. Require 4–6 scenes and a compiled duration of 15–25 seconds. Begin with `hook`, include a real `product-reveal` or `product-moment`, include a `verified-result`, and end with `closing`.

Launch copy should communicate the benefit in the first two beats. Show one or two product moments rather than a compressed feature list. Use stronger type hierarchy, short transitions and gentle camera movement. Do not simulate frame-perfect clicks; capture timing is approximate.
